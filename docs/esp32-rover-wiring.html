ESP32 rover wiring

Feather ESP32-S3 Reverse TFT (5691), ArduSimple simpleRTK2B Budget with the XBee in its socket, LIS3DH, Pololu S9V11F5, 10,000 mAh LiPo. Firmware default pins.

All connections

Every wire on one page. Pin positions on the Feather's edges are on page 4; physically, both 3V pads and GND are on the left edge (2nd, 3rd and 4th from the top), so the LIS3DH's VIN and GND wires go to the left edge, and BAT is at the top of the right edge.

1. Signal wiring

The XBee stays in the ArduSimple's socket. The ZED's UART2 is switched off (ubx_config_set.py --role rover), so the ESP32 drives the XBee's DIN through the header pin marked XBEE RX / GPS TX2 and forwards the corrections to the ZED over UART1. Build: feather_s3_reversetft_socket.

2. Power wiring

ArduSimple switch down (IOREF=N/C / 5V=INPUT): 5 V comes from the regulator and IOREF from the Feather's 3V. The XBee is powered by the socket. Leave the S9V11F5's EN unconnected; its pull-up keeps it on. Remove the 10 kΩ test resistor on the RX2 pin.

3. Feather pins

The Feather seen from the header side, USB-C at the top. GPIO 9 (SLEEP_RQ) is unused with the XBee in the socket. A0 (GPIO 18) to the regulator's EN pad is optional, only for cutting the GPS and XBee power in deep sleep (-DGPS_POWER_PIN=18, the same pin as the carrier board).

4. GPS link detail

ArduSimple names TX1 and RX1 from the ZED's side: TX1 is the ZED's output and goes to the Feather's RX.

Set up beforehand, from a computer

  • ZED: python3 ubx_config_set.py --port <ZED port> --role rover (UART1 UBX at 115200, UART2 off, saved to flash). A Pi rover uses --role rover-pi instead.
  • XBee: BD = 115200, SM = 0 (no SLEEP_RQ line in the socket).
  • Firmware: pio run -e feather_s3_reversetft_socket -t upload
